💡 PSLE Paper 2 Core Traps & Cheatsheet

1. S&T Proximity Rule (就近/就远原则)
• Neither... nor / Either... or: Verb agrees with the CLOSER subject.
• Together with / As well as: Verb agrees with the FIRST subject.

2. Reported Speech Backshift
• Simple present ➔ Simple past (is ➔ was, know ➔ knew).
• Modal shifts (can ➔ could, will ➔ would). Drop question mark in Wh- questions!

3. Editing Double Consonants
• Accommodation (2 c's, 2 m's), Committee (cc, tt, ee), Occurrence (cc, rr), Possession (ss, ss).
