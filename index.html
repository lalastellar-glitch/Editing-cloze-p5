💡 PSLE Paper 2 Core Traps & Cheatsheet

1. S&T Subject-Verb Agreement Traps
• Neither... nor / Either... or: Verb agrees with the CLOSER subject.
• Together with / As well as / Including: Verb agrees with the FIRST subject.

2. Reported Speech Backshift
• Simple present ➔ Simple past (is ➔ was, did arrive ➔ had arrived).
• Modal shifts (can ➔ could, will ➔ would). Drop '?' and use statement word order!

3. Top Editing Spelling Mnemonics
• Accommodation: 2 Cats (cc) + 2 Monkeys (mm).
• Committee: 2 Members (mm) + 2 Teas (tt) + 2 Eggs (ee).
• Embarrassed: 2 Red faces (rr) + 2 Shy smiles (ss).
• Definitely: Contains 'finite' (NO 'a').
